Our Story

About Lumiere

A place built on passion, patience, and the pursuit of the perfect meal.

The Chef

Sarah Jenkins

Head Chef & Founder

Sarah trained in Lyon before spending six years in Paris at a Michelin-starred bistro. When she returned home, she brought with her an unwavering respect for ingredients and a conviction that fine dining should feel warm, not stiff.

She opened Lumiere in 2018 with a simple mission: to create a restaurant where every guest feels genuinely cared for, and every plate is a quiet expression of joy.

01

Local Sourcing

Every ingredient is sourced within 150 miles of our kitchen. We work with over 40 local farms and producers who share our values of sustainability and quality.

02

Seasonal Menus

Our menu changes with the seasons - not as a gimmick, but because nature's produce at its peak needs very little to be extraordinary. We follow its lead.

03

Minimal Intervention

We believe in letting ingredients speak. Techniques exist to enhance, not dominate. This philosophy of restraint defines every dish that leaves our kitchen.

04

Warm Hospitality

Fine dining should feel warm, not formal. We train every team member to be genuinely present, attentive, and kind - because that is great hospitality.
